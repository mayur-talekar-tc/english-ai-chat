About BhashaAI

Empowering millions of Indians to learn English confidently through AI-powered conversations in their native language.

🎯 Our Mission

India has 22 official languages and hundreds of dialects, yet English remains the gateway to better opportunities. BhashaAI bridges this gap by letting users learn English naturally — in their own language. Whether you speak Marathi, Hindi, Gujarati, Tamil, or any other Indian language, BhashaAI understands you and teaches you English the way a friend would.
