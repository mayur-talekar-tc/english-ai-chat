Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use BhashaAI, we collect the following information:

  • • Account information (name, email) when you register
  • • Chat messages you send (to provide AI responses)
  • • Learning progress and XP data
  • • Device and browser information for analytics

2. How We Use Your Information

  • • To provide personalized English learning experiences
  • • To track your learning progress and maintain streaks
  • • To improve our AI language detection and teaching quality
  • • To send important updates about your account

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on encrypted servers. Chat messages are processed by our AI in real-time and are not shared with third parties. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Groq AI for language processing. Your chat messages are sent to Groq's API for generating responses. We do not sell or share your personal data with advertisers or other third parties.

5. Your Rights

  • • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time
  • • You can clear your chat history from the app
  • • You can request a copy of your data by emailing us

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at privacy@bhashaai.com
